How to Use

01
Sign in to PlayStation.com

Log in with your normal PlayStation account in any browser tab.

02
Get your session token

In the same browser, open ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie ↗

03
Copy the 64-character NPSSO token

Copy only the string inside {"npsso": "..."}.

04
Paste & Fetch

Paste the token into the field and click CONNECT & FETCH. Your games will be organized into PS5/PS4 owned titles and PS Plus claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe to use?
Yes, 100%. This tool is completely open source and runs entirely on your local machine. You never enter your PlayStation password. Your NPSSO token is only transmitted directly to Sony's official OAuth servers over encrypted HTTPS, and is never logged, stored on external servers, or shared with third parties.
How does it work?
It uses Sony's official OAuth 2.0 gateway. The 64-character NPSSO token exchanges for a temporary access code, which generates a short-lived bearer token. It then queries Sony's official GraphQL catalog (getPurchasedGames) and activity history APIs to separate perpetual digital purchases from subscription-based PS Plus claims.
Can my PlayStation account get banned?
No. The requests made are standard, read-only API calls—the exact same requests made by the official PlayStation Mobile App and the web store to display your library.
Where is my library data saved?
All records are saved strictly on your local disk as psn-game-library.json and psn-game-library.csv. No telemetry or external cloud storage is used.
